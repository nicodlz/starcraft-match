the important EDX preservation.

## ABI and validation

next pointers and ends null. Plain RET, no stack arguments or cleanup. EDX
and all other general-purpose registers remain unchanged. The `__fastcall`
C90 candidate has identical bytes and therefore the same observed register
effects. The caller consumes the full result, not merely AL.
