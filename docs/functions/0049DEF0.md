`ad6b58b27b8948845ccfa69bcfcc1b10d6aa7a27a371ee3e61453925288c6a46`. The complete region is
`[0x0049DEF0, 0x0049DEF5)`.

The preceding routine returns at `0x0049DEE1`, followed by INT3 through
`0x0049DEEF`. This callback returns at `0x0049DEF2` with twelve-byte cleanup;
trailing INT3 begins at `0x0049DEF5` and ends at `0x0049DEFF`.

Caller `0x004A0144` pushes this callback address, then calls `0x00414560` at
`0x004A0149`. That routine forwards the callback through a stack argument to
`0x00413D10` at `0x00414607`. The inspected dispatcher pushes three DWORD
arguments and invokes `[EBP+8]` at `0x00413D7A` and `0x00413D99`. This
corroborates the callback entry and stack cleanup beyond alignment alone.

## ABI and validation

The callback ignores all three DWORD stack arguments and returns full EAX=0.
RET 12 removes those arguments; other general-purpose registers are preserved.
The dispatcher has no caller-side argument cleanup after invoking it and forwards
the EAX result through its own return. The independently authored `__stdcall`
C90 candidate has this same register and stack contract. Argument names/types
are neutral views of ignored DWORDs, not semantic claims.
