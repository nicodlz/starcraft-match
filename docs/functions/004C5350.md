## Observed ABI and reconstruction

The body takes no observable input, touches no memory, sets full EAX to 1,
and returns with plain RET. Other general-purpose registers remain unchanged.
The dispatcher immediately tests full EAX at `0x0048919D`, corroborating the
result width. The pure C candidate returns unsigned integer 1 with no arguments;
its compiled body preserves the same registers and stack behavior.

Both existing profiles were tested. `clang-i686-scaffold` emits exactly 6 bytes;
`clang-i686-size` emits 4 unequal bytes. The complete original region and entire
isolated candidate section were compared without trimming or normalization using
`PE.region`, `coff_function`, and `compare`. Private reports remain under
`.local/exact-growth/004C5350/`. A coordinator rebuild is required before
integration; original differential execution was not performed.
